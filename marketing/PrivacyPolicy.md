# EAR privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

EAR is designed so that your music and notes never leave your device.

**What EAR collects:** nothing. EAR has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not contact any server.

**Audio you import** is copied into EAR's own storage on your device (`Documents/Studies`) so it can be analysed and played back. Your original file is not changed. Delete a study in the Notebook to remove its copy.

**Microphone:** used only when you tap Capture, for up to 30 seconds. The recording is analysed on the device and kept only if you choose Finish & study.

**Notes, measurements and progress** are stored on your device in `Documents/Studies/notebook.json`. They are included in your device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings.

**Links:** the reference-manual links in Settings open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

**Children:** EAR does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the support link on the App Store page.
