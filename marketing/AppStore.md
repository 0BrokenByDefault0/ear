## Description (≤ 4000)
EAR is a listening instrument for producers, mix engineers and curious listeners. Bring a song you love. EAR measures it on your iPhone or iPad, explains what the numbers can and cannot tell you, and turns them into small experiments for your own sessions.

MEASURE HONESTLY
• Integrated and short-term loudness in LUFS (ITU-R BS.1770-4), loudness range, and a loudness-over-time graph
• See how loudness-normalizing services would turn the master up or down
• True peak in dBTP, 4× oversampled, with a headroom note
• Key estimate with its Camelot code, and a tempo you can tap, halve or double
• Third-octave spectrum, stereo correlation, side energy, low-end width, transients and decay
Every finding is labelled as a measurement, an interpretation or a listening hypothesis. EAR never claims to identify a plug-in or a vocal take.

LISTEN CLOSELY
• Sample-accurate phrase loops: tap a section or mark your own start and end
• True mono fold-down to hear what survives on a phone speaker
• A waveform you can scrub, with section markers
• The transport stays with you inside every lesson

PRACTISE IN YOUR DAW
• Nine production lenses: drums, vocal layers, delay, reverb, stereo, arrangement, low end, dynamics and texture
• 36 step-by-step experiments with routing and plug-in names for Studio Pro or Logic Pro
• Each ends with a listening check and what to try if it fails
• Delay-time calculator from quarter notes to triplets

KEEP WHAT YOU LEARN
• A notebook of studies with notes, search by title, key or note, and sorting
• Share a designed study card or a full text report
• Track the experiments you've tried across every study

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No upload. No analytics. No cloud AI. Your audio and notes stay on your device, and your original files are never changed.

Works with unprotected WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A/AAC, FLAC and CAF files from 3 seconds to 15 minutes. Streaming-service tracks and DRM-protected downloads cannot be analysed. Includes Afterglow, an original instrumental, so you can start right away.
